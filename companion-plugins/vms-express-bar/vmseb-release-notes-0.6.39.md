# VMS Express Bar 0.6.39

- Replaces the top-floating selected-item summary with a normal in-flow summary.
- Makes **Review Order** a compact bottom purchase bar on desktop and mobile, with safe-area spacing and shell-boundary visibility so it does not obstruct navigation or unrelated page controls.
- Shows the real WooCommerce cart item count and total in the persistent bar.
- Keeps **Review Order** available when tickets or any other products are already in the WooCommerce cart, even with zero Express Bar selections or after the Express Bar ordering window closes.
- Preserves staged Express Bar selection handling, pickup name validation, age gating, availability checks, event metadata, and existing cart contents.
- Bumps the plugin header, `VMSEB_VERSION`, and `vms-build.txt` to `0.6.39`.
